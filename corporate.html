Why chair massage

Your team carries a lot. Mostly in their shoulders.

A simple perk people actually use, and remember

Desk work, long shifts, and deadline weeks all land in the same places: neck, shoulders, upper back, and hands. A short, focused chair massage targets exactly those spots, with no oils, no changing, and no time off the clock beyond a quick break.

Relief for desk-bound bodies

Focused work on the neck, shoulders, back, arms, and hands — the areas screens and long hours wear down the most.

Fits into a workday

How it works
